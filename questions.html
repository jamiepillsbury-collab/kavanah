Shabbat evening: the Amidah

Avot v'Imahot

Hebrew: Siddur Ashkenaz, Shabbat, Maariv, Amidah, Patriarchs (The Metsudah siddur, 1981, CC-BY)

  1. CBB siddur Mashiv HaRuach p. 36: Adonai s'fatai (line 1) matches.
  2. CBB siddur Mashiv HaRuach p. 38 matches these lines (imahot, g'ulah, v'ezrat Sarah).
  3. Hebrew base: Metsudah siddur (traditional). Reform wording added from Mishkan T'filah as I know it; please check each marked line, and the vowels of v'imoteinu and v'imahot (no Sefaria source for those two words).
  4. English is the traditional Metsudah translation, so it doesn't mention the matriarchs. Add your own if you like.
  5. The Ten Days of Repentance insert (Zochreinu) is left out.
  6. Line 2, Reform change: Adds v'imoteinu (and our mothers). Confirm against Mishkan T'filah.
  7. Line 3, Reform change: Adds Elohei Sarah, Elohei Rivkah, Elohei Rachel v'Elohei Leah. Confirm against Mishkan T'filah.
  8. Line 5, Reform change: Adds v'imahot (and mothers). Confirm against Mishkan T'filah.
  9. Line 5, Reform change: G'ulah (redemption) instead of go'el (a redeemer). Confirm against Mishkan T'filah.
  10. Line 7, Reform change: Adds v'ezrat Sarah (and Sarah's help). Confirm against Mishkan T'filah.
