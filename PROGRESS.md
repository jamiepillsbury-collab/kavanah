## Proposed phases
1. App shell: PWA (manifest, service worker, offline), lesson engine, reveal-on-tap translit/translation with fade, local progress save, data schema (JSON). Content: Alef-Bet letters + vowels (generated by script).
2. First blessings (e.g. Barchu, Sh'ma & V'ahavta) — Sefaria text, Reform marks, original translations, audio slots.
3. Shabbat evening service core, in Mishkan T'filah order.
4. Torah service, holidays; Mishkan HaNefesh.
5. Prayer & Meditation tab.
Deploy to GitHub Pages at end of phase 1.
